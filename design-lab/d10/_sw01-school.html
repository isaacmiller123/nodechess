Your move. Put a second attacker on f7.

Viktoryour coach

Good. The bishop stands on c4 and stares at f7. That square is defended by the king and by nothing else. One defender is not a wall.

f7 is the only square in Black’s camp that his king has to guard alone. The Italian Game is built to point at it: knight to f3, bishop to c4, and the king still the only guard.

Two attackers against one defender is not a trick. It is arithmetic, and it is why he just moved a knight in front of that pawn instead of defending it.

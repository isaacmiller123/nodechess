Eight alternate dark palettes

Same content in every panel, so the only variable is colour. Each palette varies the accent hue and the neutral temperature together, because a cold grey with a warm accent is a different object from a warm grey with a cold accent. Type, scale, spacing and radii are d01's, unchanged. Ratios below are computed, not estimated.

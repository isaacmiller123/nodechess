nodechess, lane C: the board as a topology

A board is already a lattice, and a lattice is already a network, so nothing here adds a network to chess. Each mark reveals the one that was in the board to begin with. Squares are addresses, intersections are where peers sit, and the diagonal is the edge that joins them. Primary marks carry detail and are for 24px and up. Each favicon is the same object with detail taken out, never a substitute idea. One flat colour throughout, no gradient, no badge.

Idea. Play happens on the points, not in the cells. The orthogonal lattice is the addressing scheme, a1 to h8, drawn as hairlines that run off the edge because any one machine only ever sees a fragment. Where a peer sits, the lattice is cut away and leaves the node standing in clean ground, so the only thing that touches a node is the heavy diagonal, which is the connection. One node is drawn open, and that one is you.
Favicon. Removed one rank of the lattice, one node, and the open ring, keeping the cut halos and the cropped diagonal that runs off both corners.

Idea. Only the dark squares of a board are drawn, and dark squares touch nothing but each other, corner to corner. That is already a connected graph, so the network is not added, it is what a chessboard was doing all along. The light squares are never drawn and become the voids that give the mark its air. At each corner meeting sits a small diamond, a link on a lattice intersection. One square in the set is missing and its link is left hanging into empty ground, because the network continues past what this node holds.
Favicon. Removed one board rank, three squares, and the hanging link, leaving five squares and four diamonds, which is the same corner-to-corner logic at its smallest.

Idea. No node holds the whole network, so the board is drawn in four states of knowing, banded along the long diagonal. Squares you hold are solid, squares you have only the outline of are drawn as outline, squares that are an address and nothing more keep two corner brackets, and squares that are only a rumour keep one. Past that, nothing, which is the honest edge of a partial view. Every state stays locked to the same lattice, so the board is still legible in the part that was never filled in, and the whole gradient is made of shape alone, no tint and no opacity.
Favicon. Removed both bracket states and one rank, keeping only solid and outline, so the dissolve survives as three held squares against three outlines.

Idea. A solid board stood on its corner, the way you draw a board when you care about its diagonals, with everything cut out rather than drawn on. Fine cuts divide it into addresses. One heavy cut runs the addresses as a staircase, forks once, and leaves by three different edges, notching the silhouette so the shape is never a badge. The widened cuts at the turns are the peers the route passed through. The mark is entirely what was taken away.
Favicon. Removed the address lattice and the widened turns, keeping the diamond and a single straightened channel with its fork, so the notched silhouette still reads.

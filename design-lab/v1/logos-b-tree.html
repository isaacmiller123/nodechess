IDEAA move lattice, not a move tree. Every edge has the same slope so the figure is crystalline, and the whole graph is identical when you turn it upside down, which is the precise statement that no node is the root. Two different routes reach the lower left node, the heavy stroke picks out the main line threading through the middle, and one node is an open ring because only one peer has been there.

REDUCTIONRemoved six of the seven nodes and six of the eight edges, down to the smallest unit the lattice is made of, one diamond with a heavy route and a light route between the same two positions.

IDEAOne move, drawn as the only straight stroke in the mark, with the whole past fanning out of one end and the whole future fanning out of the other, so every position is a leaf of what came before and a root of what comes after and neither fan is privileged. Two strands cross on the way up because two move orders swap, two more arrive at a single shared tip, and one tip is an open ring because nobody else has that line.

REDUCTIONRemoved both fans down to a single strand each way and dropped every terminal node, leaving the heavy move and the two sweeps that prove it has a before and an after.

Three pieces out, and Black has answered with the same bishop on the same diagonal. Symmetry is not a plan. Whoever breaks it first has the game.

Your bishop on c4 already stares at f7. What it does not have is a pawn beside it. Play c3 so that d4 comes next with support, and Black's bishop on c5 has to answer a question instead of asking one.

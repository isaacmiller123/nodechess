# d02

FEEL: Standing table of contents
Intuitiveness: immediate. Every destination is a word you can already read, in one
list that never collapses, never expands on hover, and holds no icons at all.
Navigation: a permanently open contents column set as book front matter, account
pasted at its foot like a bookplate; the section you are in opens its own
sub-entries in place, so the list annotates itself by position instead of hiding
anything behind a control.
Motion: almost none, only a colour settling in 120ms; nothing slides or fades in.
Mobile: the column lies down. Five words to a bottom bar with 60px targets, the
full contents becomes a tapable list on home, running head stays.

STYLE: Warm graphite, gilt rule
Palette: #121110 page and #1e1c19 surface, a warm graphite carrying just enough
red-yellow to stop reading as slate; #e8e1d7 text, warm off-white for long prose;
#c8a45c gilt, the only accent, off a stamped spine; #c4626a rubric rose for the
annotation mark and for danger; #8fa85a sap green for success. The warmth is
pigment, never a beige field.
Type: body is a serif (Source Serif 4, falling back through Charter and Iowan Old
Style to Georgia), because prose is the material. Display is that family at
display size, used once per screen at weight 400, never a poster headline. Mono
(IBM Plex Mono) carries every label, running head, action and move, because in
chess literature the display material IS notation. Mono for headings, serif for
reading: that inversion is the pairing.
Fonts: nothing loads over the network; the stacks resolve today to
Charter/Iowan/Georgia and SF Mono/Menlo, close in width and mood. To ship:
SourceSerif4-Variable.woff2, SourceSerif4Display-Variable.woff2,
IBMPlexMono-Regular/Medium.woff2, all OFL, self-hosted with real @font-face.
Icons: Feather Icons, MIT, 1.5px stroke, exactly two (search, arrow-right).
Nothing in navigation is an icon: a pictogram for Crazyhouse is a guess.
Signature: the 2px gilt rule in the left margin. It marks the section you are in,
the row under your cursor, the lesson you are on, and Viktor himself.

Combines well with: 05, verified by swap, monochrome mono is quiet the same way
and my structure needs no colour to stay legible; 07, verified by swap, its navy
and sans read as a different product on the same bones.
Fights with: any style spending high chroma on large fills, since my one filled
control becomes a shout in a room built of rules and space, and any style with no
distinct mono face, which flattens the label voice into the body serif.

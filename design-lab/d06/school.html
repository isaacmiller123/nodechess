Your coach

Bishop to c4. Two of your pieces look at f7 now, not one. Most players point that bishop at nothing and wonder why the attack never arrives.

Black's f7 pawn is guarded by the king and nobody else. Your knight on f3 and your bishop on c4 both aim at it, so every plan Black chooses has to answer that first.

Black will defend with Bc5 or Nf6. Both are respectable. What you may not do is hand that bishop off for a knight and call it development.

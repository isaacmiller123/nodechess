# d06

FEEL: Lid stack navigation
Intuitiveness: learnable. Every destination is visible and labeled at all times, so
nothing has to be found, but that a back edge comes forward when you click it is
something you learn on the first try rather than know on sight.
Navigation: four sections are stacked box lids, the one you are in is the front lid
filling the screen, the other three behind it as labeled edges. No menus, no rail.
Motion: objects lift on a short spring and settle, and every control presses into the
surface when pushed. Nothing slides sideways.
Mobile: depth cannot be read one handed, so it becomes press depth. The four sections
are keys along the bottom, the current one pressed into the case, and the home board
shrinks to a cover so the clock and the start key stay above the fold.

STYLE: Walnut, felt and brass
Palette (default walnut, plus slate, oxblood and birch, all dark):
  #1a130e page, the shadow under a shelf
  #30241b surface, the walnut plank every panel is cut from
  #4e3b2c border, the edge where two pieces of wood meet
  #d2a24c accent, brass hardware, used only on the current thing and the one real action
  #2c4436 felt, the recessed mats that hold boards and printed labels
  #f2e7d8 text, bone
Type: display Zilla Slab, body Source Sans 3, mono IBM Plex Mono, all SIL OFL and all
named as files to self host in style.css. None is installed here, so the stacks fall
through to Rockwell then Georgia for the slab and Segoe UI then Helvetica Neue for the
body, which is what these screenshots show. A slab because box lids are printed, not
rendered, and Plex Mono because its squared terminals match the slab in notation.
Icons: Phosphor, Fill weight, MIT. Solid shapes have weight where hairline strokes look
drawn on. Inlined as one SVG sprite per page, no font and no CDN.
Signature: the shelf. Each family of games stands on a real plank with a highlight on
its front lip and a shadow under it, boxes lift off it on hover, and a family with two
titles leaves the rest of the plank empty. Behind that, one rule governs everything:
a single light source high and left, so every surface has a lighter top edge token and
a darker falling edge token, and the material reads without a single texture.

Combines well with: 01 and 07. Both are cool, low chroma and single accent, so my
depth machinery still reads once their colors take over. Verified by swapping both.
Fights with: any feel built on a persistent side rail, since my style spends its
contrast budget on one large surface, and any style with zero radius and hairline
borders, which flattens the lids into tabs.
